Mind & body

Movement, stress management, and lifestyle approaches with evidence behind them.

Strong evidence
Exercise & resistance training
Aerobic and resistance exercise both improve insulin sensitivity in PCOS — independently of weight loss. RCTs show 12 weeks of structured exercise restores ovulation in a significant proportion of participants.
Learn more
Good evidence
Stress management
Chronic stress drives cortisol elevation, which dysregulates the HPA-HPG axis — worsening PCOS hormonal imbalance. Mindfulness and stress reduction interventions show measurable hormonal benefits in trials.
Good evidence
Yoga
Multiple RCTs show yoga reduces androgens, LH, and cortisol in PCOS — likely via stress reduction and improved insulin sensitivity. Particularly beneficial for anxiety and mood symptoms.
Moderate evidence
Sleep improvement
Poor sleep quality is common in PCOS and worsens insulin resistance. Observational data suggest sleep extension improves androgen and cortisol patterns — interventional data is limited.
Moderate evidence
Acupuncture
Several small RCTs suggest acupuncture may improve menstrual frequency and reduce LH levels. Mechanistic evidence is plausible (neuroendocrine pathways) but sample sizes remain small.
