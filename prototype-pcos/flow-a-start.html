Supplements Strong evidence
Myo-inositol
The most studied supplement for PCOS. Multiple meta-analyses show it improves insulin sensitivity, restores menstrual regularity, and reduces androgen levels — particularly the 40:1 myo:d-chiro ratio.

Diet Strong evidence
Low-GI diet
Choosing lower-glycaemic foods reduces insulin spikes — the core metabolic driver of PCOS symptoms for most people. RCTs consistently show improvements in fasting insulin, testosterone, and menstrual regularity.
Learn more
Mind & Body Strong evidence
Exercise & resistance training
Both aerobic and resistance exercise improve insulin sensitivity in PCOS — independently of weight loss. RCTs show 12 weeks of structured exercise restores ovulation in a significant proportion of participants.
