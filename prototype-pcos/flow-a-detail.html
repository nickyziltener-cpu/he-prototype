What it is

Inositol is a naturally occurring sugar alcohol that plays a critical role in insulin signalling. In many people with PCOS, there's a defect in how cells use inositol — which impairs the insulin signalling pathway and drives insulin resistance.

Supplementing restores this pathway without the gastrointestinal side effects of metformin. The 40:1 myo:d-chiro-inositol ratio reflects the physiological ratio found in healthy cells — and outperforms myo-inositol alone in clinical trials.

How it's used
1
Choose the right form
Powder dissolves in water and absorbs better than capsules. Look for the 40:1 combined ratio (myo-inositol + d-chiro-inositol).
2
Split into two doses
2g morning + 2g evening, both dissolved in water. Consistent daily use is what drives results — irregular use doesn't work.
3
Give it 12 weeks
Most cycle changes appear between weeks 8–12. Track your cycle start date — this is your primary indicator of progress.
What the research shows
Meta-analysis · 2022 · 14 RCTs · n = 1,234
Myo-inositol significantly improved menstrual regularity (OR 3.8), reduced fasting insulin (SMD –0.72), and lowered testosterone levels compared with placebo.
RCT · 2021 · n = 120 · 24 weeks
The 40:1 myo:d-chiro-inositol combination outperformed myo-inositol alone in restoring ovulation (78% vs 62%). Both significantly better than placebo.
What to expect week by week
Weeks 1–4Possible mild digestive adjustment. No immediate hormonal changes expected.
Weeks 4–8Some women notice improved energy or reduced bloating. Insulin markers improving internally.
Weeks 8–12Menstrual regularity changes most likely to emerge in this window. Track your cycle carefully.
Beyond 12 weeksContinued use associated with progressive hormonal improvement. Many women use indefinitely.
Practical notes

Buy from reputable brands with third-party testing — inositol is a supplement, not a drug, so quality varies. Rare side effects include nausea and loose stools at higher doses. If you take blood sugar medication, discuss with your GP — combined use may lower glucose more than intended.
