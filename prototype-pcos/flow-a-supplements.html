Supplements

Evidence-graded supplements with demonstrated PCOS benefit — ranked by research quality.

Strong evidence
Myo-inositol
Multiple meta-analyses confirm improvements in insulin resistance, menstrual regularity, and androgen levels. Best taken as a 40:1 myo:d-chiro-inositol combination (4g daily).
Learn more
Good evidence
Spearmint tea
Two RCTs show significant reductions in free and total testosterone after 30 days. Particularly useful for androgen-driven symptoms — acne, hirsutism, hair thinning.
Good evidence
N-Acetyl Cysteine (NAC)
A precursor to glutathione with insulin-sensitising effects comparable to metformin in several RCTs. Also shows benefit for ovulation induction in fertility-focused studies.
Good evidence
Vitamin D
Deficiency is highly prevalent in PCOS and correlates with worse metabolic markers. Correction improves insulin sensitivity and may support menstrual regularity in deficient women.
Moderate evidence
Zinc
Several small RCTs suggest benefits for acne and hirsutism in PCOS via anti-androgenic mechanisms. Effect sizes are modest.
Moderate evidence
Berberine
Comparable metabolic effects to metformin in several RCTs. Reduces insulin resistance, testosterone, and LH levels. Limited long-term safety data — discuss with GP before starting.
Moderate evidence
Omega-3 fatty acids
Anti-inflammatory benefits with supportive evidence for reducing testosterone and triglycerides in PCOS. Effect sizes smaller than inositol.
