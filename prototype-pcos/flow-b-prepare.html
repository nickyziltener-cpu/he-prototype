Getting ready for myo-inositol
Before you start, here's what to buy, when to begin, and what to watch for in your cycle.
◯ Cycle timing
You can start myo-inositol at any point in your cycle — there's no need to wait for day 1. Take it continuously, every day, regardless of where you are in your cycle.
What to buy
🧪
Myo-inositol powder (40:1 ratio)
Look for a product that combines myo-inositol and d-chiro-inositol at a 40:1 ratio — this is the clinically studied combination. Total myo-inositol: 4g/day. D-chiro: 100mg/day.
⚗️
Third-party tested brands
Inositol is not regulated as a drug. Choose brands with independent quality testing. Powder absorbs better than capsules based on trial evidence.
💧
How to take it
Dissolve in water. Split into two doses — morning and evening — for more consistent blood levels throughout the day.
What to expect
  • Weeks 1–4: Possible mild digestive adjustment. No visible hormonal changes yet.
  • Weeks 4–8: Energy may improve. Insulin sensitivity improving internally. Note any mood changes.
  • Weeks 8–12: This is when menstrual changes are most likely. Track your cycle carefully.
  • Beyond 12 weeks: Many women continue indefinitely — sustained benefit builds over time.
What to track
📅
Cycle length
Log when your period starts each time. Cycle length is the primary indicator of ovulation returning.
💆
PCOS symptoms
Log acne, hair growth, energy, mood, and bloating. These give you signal beyond just the cycle.
⚡
Energy
Insulin sensitivity changes often show up as energy and mood improvements before the cycle changes.
