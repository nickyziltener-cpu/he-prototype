Anti-inflammatory diet

What it is

Endometriosis is an inflammatory condition — and what you eat can shift the balance of prostaglandins that drive pain. This isn't an elimination diet. It's a gradual dietary shift: more anti-inflammatory foods over 8 weeks, less of the foods that promote inflammation. No strict rules, just a direction to move in.

Perfection isn't the goal. Gradual shifts in the right direction — logged consistently — is what lets us see what's working.

How long it takes

Studies typically run 8 weeks before evaluating results. Inflammation takes time to shift — stick with it through a full two cycles before drawing conclusions.

Consider a dietitian
A dietitian can tailor the approach to your specific symptoms and help you build habits that stick. Ask your GP for an NHS referral, or search for a dietitian with experience in chronic pain or women's health.
