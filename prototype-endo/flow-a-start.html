Mind & Body Strong evidence
Pelvic floor physiotherapy
Specialist physiotherapy targeting pelvic muscle tension — a key driver of endo-related pain. Multiple trials show significant pain and quality-of-life improvement, comparable to hormonal treatments.

Diet Moderate evidence
Anti-inflammatory diet
Reducing pro-inflammatory foods — red meat, processed foods, trans fats — while increasing omega-3s and plant foods. Observational research consistently links diet quality to endo risk and severity; RCT evidence for the dietary pattern itself is still limited.
Learn more
Supplements Good evidence
N-acetyl cysteine (NAC)
An antioxidant supplement shown in an Italian RCT to meaningfully reduce endometrioma size and pain — with 21 of 47 women cancelling planned surgery after 3 months of use.
