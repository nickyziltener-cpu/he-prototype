What to increase — anti-inflammatory foods

Perfection isn't the goal — gradual, logged shifts are what let us see what's working for you specifically.

Give it time

Studies run for 8 weeks before evaluating results. You're in week 2 — inflammation takes time to shift. Keep logging each week and we'll review together at week 8.

Getting support
A dietitian can tailor this to your symptoms
Dietary changes for endo are more effective with professional guidance — someone who understands the link between inflammation and chronic pelvic pain.
NHS dietitian referral
Ask your GP for a referral — mention you have endometriosis and are interested in an anti-inflammatory dietary approach. Free on NHS.
Find a specialist privately
Look for a dietitian with experience in chronic pain or women's health. Private sessions from ~£80.
