Prepare for
Heat therapy

What to do

Apply a heat pad or hot water bottle to your lower abdomen for 20–30 minutes. Use it daily during flares — or as part of a daily routine. Some people combine heat with a TENS device for stronger relief; either alone is a valid starting point.

Where to get it
Most pharmacies & online retailers
No prescription needed · reusable heat pads are typically £15–35 · TENS devices are optional (£25–50).
Heat pad £15–35 (one-off) TENS optional
When to expect results

Heat works acutely — you may feel relief within minutes. We track 4 weeks to see how your symptoms shift across a full cycle, so you can judge overall impact rather than day-to-day variation.

Keep your GP in the loop
Heat therapy is safe and well tolerated. If you use a TENS device, mention it at your next visit — especially if you have any implanted devices (e.g. a pacemaker or hormonal coil with embedded electronics).
