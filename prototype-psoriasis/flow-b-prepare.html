Trial 1 Topical

Your moisturising routine

Here's everything you need to know to make this trial count.

What you'll do
1
Apply emollient twice daily
Morning and night — to your whole body, not just affected areas. Use a generous amount. Ointments are most effective; creams work better for daytime use.
2
Apply to damp skin after bathing
Pat yourself dry — leaving skin slightly damp — then apply within 3 minutes. This locks in moisture rather than sitting on top of dry skin.
3
Apply before any prescription topicals
Wait 30 minutes after emollient before applying steroids or vitamin D analogues. This isn't about absorption — it's about not diluting or washing away the active treatment.
What to look for
Ointments (most effective)
Epaderm ointment · 50:50 white soft paraffin/liquid paraffin · Diprobase ointment
Creams (better for daytime)
Doublebase · E45 · Cetraben cream · Aveeno
Ask your GP for a prescription
Many of these are prescribable — one flat prescription charge, regardless of product size. Worth mentioning at your next appointment.
How you'll track it

Each evening, you'll log a quick check-in. It takes about 20 seconds.

✓ Did you apply emollient today?
✓ How's your skin compared to yesterday?
✓ Any specific symptoms today?
For your dermatologist
Your logs make your appointments more useful

After 4–6 weeks you'll have a symptom trend to share. This gives your specialist concrete data rather than a rough sense of "it's been okay, I think".
