Topical treatments

8 options · 5 with Strong evidence. Includes OTC options a dermatologist may not mention.

ACCESS: Rx — prescription OTC — pharmacy Clinic
Emollients & moisturising
Strong evidence OTC
Daily moisturising reduces scaling, relieves itch, and significantly reduces the amount of topical treatment needed. Foundation of all psoriasis management. Simple, safe, evidence-graded.
Learn more
Topical corticosteroids
Strong evidence Rx
First-line treatment for most mild-to-moderate psoriasis. Reduce inflammation and slow skin cell turnover. Potency matched to site and severity. Prescribed by your GP or dermatologist.
Vitamin D analogues (calcipotriol)
Strong evidence Rx
Calcipotriol slows skin cell division via vitamin D receptor binding. Widely used alone and as the basis of the most evidence-backed combination product. Doesn't cause skin thinning. Well tolerated for long-term maintenance.
Calcipotriol + betamethasone (Dovobet / Enstilar)
Strong evidence Rx
The combination outperforms either ingredient alone — 73% PASI reduction vs 49% for calcipotriol and 63% for steroid separately. Available as ointment (Dovobet), gel (Xamiol), or aerosol foam (Enstilar). The best-evidenced topical in psoriasis by network meta-analysis. If you're using these as separate products, ask your dermatologist about the combination.
Coal tar preparations
Moderate evidence OTC Rx
One of the oldest psoriasis treatments, still widely used — particularly for scalp. Anti-proliferative and anti-inflammatory. Network meta-analysis found body-plaque efficacy no better than placebo; evidence is stronger for scalp formulations. Available OTC (medicated shampoos) and by prescription at higher concentrations.
Dithranol (anthralin)
Good evidence Rx
Effective for chronic plaque psoriasis. Applied for short contact periods (10–30 minutes). Can stain skin and clothing. Less commonly used due to inconvenience, but well-evidenced.
Phototherapy (UVB / PUVA)
Strong evidence Clinic
Narrowband UVB is a dermatologist-led treatment for moderate-to-severe plaque psoriasis — very well evidenced. Delivered in outpatient sessions. PUVA (UV + psoralen) is reserved for specific cases.
Scalp-specific treatments
Strong evidence OTC Rx
The Cochrane review of scalp psoriasis (59 RCTs, n=11,561) found calcipotriol-betamethasone gel/foam is the most effective topical for the scalp — superior to either ingredient alone. Targeted treatment dramatically improves quality of life. If scalp is your most distressing site, ask specifically about Xamiol gel or Enstilar foam.
Salicylic acid (keratolytic)
Moderate evidence OTC
Descales thick plaques and enhances penetration of other topicals — applying before steroids or vitamin D analogues helps them reach deeper layers. Evidence is as an enabler rather than standalone treatment. No placebo-controlled trials for solo use. Widely available in body washes and lotions.
Mahonia aquifolium (Oregon grape — topical)
Moderate evidence OTC
An herbal extract applied topically to psoriatic plaques. Three published clinical trials and two systematic reviews support efficacy — a 2013 meta-analysis found a relative risk of 3.37 vs placebo across 5 studies. Almost never discussed in NHS settings. Available as Reliéva or similar OTC herbal creams. Genuinely under-discussed.
