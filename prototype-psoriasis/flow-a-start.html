Topical Strong evidence
Emollients & moisturising
Daily moisturising is one of the most evidence-backed steps for psoriasis. Consistent use reduces scaling, relieves itch, and significantly lowers the amount of topical treatment needed. The simpler the formulation, the better tolerated.

Mind & Body Good evidence
Stress management (MBSR)
Mindfulness-Based Stress Reduction has good clinical evidence for psoriasis — partly because stress is both a trigger and a response to flares. Kabat-Zinn's landmark study showed faster plaque clearing in patients who combined MBSR with phototherapy.
Learn more
Diet Good evidence
Mediterranean-style diet
The 2025 MEDIPSO randomised trial found 47% of participants following this diet achieved 75% skin improvement versus 0% in controls. An anti-inflammatory eating pattern with the best trial evidence of any dietary intervention for psoriasis.
