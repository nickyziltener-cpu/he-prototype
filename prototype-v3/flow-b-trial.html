What to do

The dose used in studies is one enteric-coated capsule, three times a day, taken before meals. Enteric coating matters — it lets the oil reach the gut rather than releasing in the stomach.

Where to get it
Most pharmacies & health shops
No prescription needed · look for "IBS peppermint" or "enteric-coated peppermint oil".
