What to avoid — elimination phase

Fermentable carbohydrates that feed gut bacteria and trigger symptoms in IBS. There are five groups to learn — it looks like a lot, but patterns emerge quickly.

Fructans
Garlic Onion Wheat Rye Spring onion Shallots
Lactose
Cow's milk Soft cheese Yogurt Ice cream
Fructose
Apples Pears Mango Honey Dried fruit
Legumes
Chickpeas Lentils Kidney beans Hummus
Polyols
Stone fruit Mushrooms Cauliflower Sugar-free gum
Safe to keep eating
Rice Oats Potatoes Eggs Meat & fish Hard cheese Lactose-free milk Carrots Bell peppers Spinach Courgette Bananas Oranges Strawberries Blueberries Sourdough

This isn't a starvation diet — most proteins, many vegetables, and several grains are fine. The Monash app has a full verified list.

Give it time

Studies run the elimination phase for 6 weeks before evaluating results. You're in week 2 — keep logging each week and we'll review together at week 6. Reintroduction then tells you exactly which foods are your personal triggers.

Getting support
A dietitian makes this significantly more effective
Studies show better outcomes with professional guidance — partly because of accuracy, partly because it reduces the anxiety of second-guessing yourself on every meal.
NHS dietitian referral
Ask your GP to refer you — mention you're trying the low FODMAP protocol for IBS. Free on NHS.
Self-refer to a FODMAP dietitian
Private sessions from ~£80. Look for "FODMAP trained" or "IBS dietitian" — not all dietitians are up to date on this protocol.
