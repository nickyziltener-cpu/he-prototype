Prepare for
Peppermint oil

What to do

The dose used in studies is one enteric-coated capsule, three times a day, taken before meals. Enteric coating matters — it lets the oil reach the gut rather than releasing in the stomach.

Where to get it
Most pharmacies & health shops
No prescription needed · look for "IBS peppermint" or "enteric-coated peppermint oil".
Typically £8–12 / month 3rd-party tested
When to expect results

Studies typically run for about 4 weeks before judging whether peppermint oil is effective. Give it the full month before deciding whether it's working for you.

Keep your GP in the loop
Peppermint oil is generally well tolerated, but mention it at your next visit — especially if you take other medication.
