Low FODMAP diet

What it is

FODMAP stands for a group of fermentable carbohydrates that feed gut bacteria and trigger symptoms in IBS. The diet works in two phases: a strict 6-week elimination to calm symptoms, then a systematic reintroduction that pinpoints exactly which foods are your personal triggers.

How long it takes

Studies run the elimination phase for 6 weeks, then 3–9 weeks for systematic reintroduction. Most people notice a meaningful change by week 3 of elimination. Reintroduction is where the real learning happens — one food group at a time.
